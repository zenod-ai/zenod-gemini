# Zenod

Zenod is the user's personal memory: notes, voice memos, emails and transcripts they sent it, filed into a Markdown vault they own on GitHub or Google Drive.

The `zenod` MCP server offers these tools: `search_memory`, `get_memory`, `store_memory`, `ingest_memory`, `get_ingest_result`, `ask_brain` and `get_task_result`. Answers from the vault cite the pages they came from.
